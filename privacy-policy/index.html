Privacy Policy

STA Community Nursing & NDIS Services
Operated by STA Connect Pty Ltd as trustee for STA Unit Trust
Policy date: 31 October 2026

Your Privacy Matters to Us

At STA Community Nursing & NDIS Services (STA), we believe that protecting your privacy is an essential part of providing safe, compassionate and person-centred care. We understand that seeking nursing, disability, allied health, aged-care-related or community support may involve sharing personal and sensitive information. We are committed to treating your information with confidentiality, care and respect.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, when it may be shared, and your rights regarding that information. Our practices are guided by applicable Australian privacy legislation, including the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), the Australian Privacy Principles and the Health Records Act 2001 (Vic). This policy applies to personal information handled through our website, enquiries, referrals and service delivery.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on your enquiry, referral or the services you receive, STA may collect:

Personal and contact information

  • Name, date of birth, address, telephone number and email address.
  • Preferred contact methods and service locations.
  • Details of carers, emergency contacts and authorised representatives.

Health and disability information

  • Relevant medical history and health conditions.
  • Disability-related information and support requirements.
  • Clinical assessments, care plans and progress notes.
  • Relevant medical reports and referral documents.
  • Information needed to arrange safe and appropriate support.

Funding and service information

  • NDIS participant details and relevant plan information.
  • Other applicable funding arrangements.
  • Service agreements, billing details and records of services delivered.

Website and enquiry information

  • Information voluntarily submitted through our enquiry and referral forms.
  • Correspondence and supporting documents provided to STA.
  • Limited technical information associated with website operation and security.

We aim to collect only information reasonably necessary for our functions and services. Sensitive information, including health and disability information, is collected with consent or where otherwise permitted or required by law.

2. How We Collect Your Information

We may collect information directly from you when you:

  • Submit an enquiry or referral.
  • Contact us by telephone, email or in person.
  • Request, arrange or receive services.
  • Complete an assessment or service agreement.
  • Provide information relevant to your care or support.

Where appropriate and permitted by law, we may also receive information from healthcare professionals, hospitals, allied health practitioners, support coordinators, authorised representatives, funding organisations and government agencies. Where practical, we collect information directly from the person concerned. If you provide information about another person, please ensure you have appropriate authority to do so.

Where lawful and practical, you may make a general enquiry without identifying yourself. However, we may need identifying information to respond to a referral, assess eligibility or arrange services.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and referrals.
  • Understand individual care and support needs.
  • Assess service suitability and availability.
  • Arrange, coordinate and deliver services.
  • Communicate with consumers, participants, families and authorised representatives.
  • Coordinate care with healthcare and allied health professionals.
  • Prepare service agreements and manage funding and billing.
  • Maintain accurate clinical and service records.
  • Respond to complaints, incidents and safety concerns.
  • Undertake appropriate quality assurance and service improvement activities.
  • Meet applicable legal, regulatory and professional obligations.

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We will only use information for another purpose where permitted by law, including where any required consent has been obtained. We do not use sensitive health or disability information for targeted advertising.

4. How We Share Your Information

STA respects the confidentiality of personal and health information. Where appropriate and permitted by law, relevant information may be shared with:

  • STA personnel and authorised workers involved in arranging or providing services.
  • Healthcare professionals and allied health practitioners involved in your care.
  • Support coordinators, plan managers and relevant funding organisations.
  • Authorised family members, guardians and representatives.
  • Government agencies, regulators and other authorities where disclosure is required or authorised by law.
  • Contracted service providers assisting with administration, technology or other legitimate business functions.

We aim to share only the information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose. Where consent is required, we will seek it before sharing your information. Information may be disclosed without consent where required or authorised by law, including in certain circumstances involving serious threats to health or safety.

5. How We Protect and Retain Your Information

STA takes reasonable steps to protect personal and sensitive information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. Our information management practices include:

  • Confidentiality requirements for personnel and contractors.
  • Access restrictions based on legitimate work responsibilities.
  • Appropriate safeguards for electronic and physical records.
  • Procedures for handling and sharing confidential information.
  • Processes for identifying and responding to privacy incidents.
  • Secure disposal or de-identification when information is no longer required and applicable retention obligations have been met.

Only people with an appropriate work-related reason and authority should access personal information. We retain information for the periods required by applicable legislation, professional obligations and our recordkeeping procedures. Although reasonable precautions are taken, no electronic communication or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Cookies and Website Analytics

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that websites may store on your device to support website functions and remember certain settings.

Essential cookies

STA’s website currently uses essential cookies only. These cookies support basic website functionality, security and features such as online enquiry forms. They are not used for targeted advertising or behavioural profiling.

Website analytics

STA may introduce basic website analytics in the future to understand how visitors use our website and identify opportunities to improve its accessibility, navigation and performance. Before introducing analytics, we will assess the information collected, relevant privacy requirements and any consent or preference controls needed. We will update this policy to reflect material changes to our website tracking practices.

Managing cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect the operation of certain website features. STA does not currently use cookies for targeted advertising or behavioural profiling.

7. Overseas Disclosure of Personal Information

STA uses technology and service providers to support its communications, information management and business operations. Some service arrangements may involve information being processed, stored or accessed outside Australia. Whether those arrangements constitute disclosure to an overseas recipient depends on the nature of the service and the controls STA retains over the information.

Where personal information is disclosed to an overseas recipient, STA will take steps required by applicable Australian privacy law. Information about likely overseas disclosures and the relevant countries, where practicable, will be provided in this policy once STA’s service-provider arrangements have been verified.

8. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

You may request access to personal information STA holds about you or ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, irrelevant or misleading. You may also ask questions about how your information is collected, used, stored or disclosed.

Where applicable, you may withdraw or change a previous consent, subject to legal and service-related requirements. We may need to verify your identity or authority before processing a request. We will respond in accordance with applicable privacy laws and explain any lawful reason for refusing access or correction. You can make a request using the contact details in Section 10.

9. Privacy Concerns and Complaints

If you have concerns about how STA has collected, used, stored or disclosed your personal information, please contact us. We take privacy concerns seriously and manage them in accordance with our complaints management procedures and applicable privacy laws. When a privacy complaint is received, we will:

  • Acknowledge the concern.
  • Review the circumstances and investigate where appropriate.
  • Consider whether corrective action is required.
  • Communicate the outcome to the person making the complaint.

We aim to respond within 30 days. If additional time is required, we will keep you informed.

If you are dissatisfied with our response, you may be able to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC). Depending on the circumstances, other relevant health, disability or aged-care complaints bodies may also be able to assist.

10. Contact STA

For privacy enquiries, requests to access or correct personal information, or concerns about how your information has been handled, please contact:

STA Community Nursing & NDIS Services
Email: support@stacommunitycare.com.au
Telephone: 0430 207 188

You can also contact us through the Contact Us page on our website. Please avoid including unnecessary sensitive health information in an initial general enquiry.

11. Our Organisational Privacy and Dignity Policy

STA maintains a broader Privacy and Dignity Policy that guides our organisational approach to confidentiality, informed consent, information management and respectful care. This policy supports our commitment to protecting the privacy, dignity and rights of people receiving our services. Our organisational policy also addresses relevant NDIS Practice Standards and information management responsibilities.

You may request further information or a copy of our organisational Privacy and Dignity Policy by contacting STA.

12. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, technology, information-handling practices or applicable legal requirements. The current version will be made available on our website, together with its date of update.

At STA, protecting your privacy is part of respecting your dignity, choices and individuality.
