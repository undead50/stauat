The STA Care Difference

Because everyone deserves care that is personal, flexible, and grounded in respect.

For years we have worked alongside individuals, families and carers across Victoria to make care kinder, transparent, and built around what matters to you.

Experience You Can Trust

Trusted by tens of thousands of families across Victoria. Decades of compassionate in-home care delivery adhering to the highest quality benchmarks.

Local & Community Centred

Dedicated regional and metro neighbourhood hubs. Your support team lives in your community, knows the local resources, and stays readily available.

NDIS Registered & Audited

Rigorous quality safeguarding, Australian clinical governance, ongoing staff credential checks, and comprehensive insurance coverage for your peace of mind.

Reliable, Consistent Care

On time, with consistent regular support workers who understand your preferences. Backed by automated roster backup so you’re never left waiting.

Person-Centred & Flexible

You choose the schedule, the goals, and the activities. Change your hours or care focus at any time without punitive administration fees.

Qualified Allied Health Network

Direct access to over 500+ nurses, occupational therapists, physiotherapists, and mental health clinicians under a unified care coordination protocol.

Why Choose STA

Care that works around you, not the other way around.

No lock-in contracts, no hidden fees, and no waiting when it matters most — just honest, transparent support you can rely on.

No Lock-In Contracts

Start, pause, or change your care at any time. There are no long-term contracts or exit fees — just support that fits your life, not the other way around.

No Hidden Management Fees

What you see is what you pay. We don’t add hidden administration or management fees that eat into your care budget.

Transparent Pricing

Clear, upfront pricing for every service, explained before you commit to anything — so you always know exactly what you’re paying for.

Urgent Care Support

Need help fast? Our team can respond to urgent care needs, not just pre-scheduled visits, so you’re never left without support when it matters most.

Detailed Care Reports

Stay informed with clear, detailed reports after every visit, so you and your family always know how care is going.
